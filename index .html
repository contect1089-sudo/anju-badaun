1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Anju Bajaranji Cal is built with a local-first architecture. Your calculations and app preferences remain stored locally on your Android device using Android Room (SQLite) and Jetpack DataStore:

  • Calculation History: Basic and scientific expressions, results, and timestamps.
  • User Preferences: Selected glassmorphism theme, sound ON/OFF state, synthesized sound pack, haptic feedback toggle, angle unit (DEG/RAD), and verified subscription status.

We do not require account registration, and we do not upload your calculation history or utility inputs (Age, Percentage, Discount, BMI) to AiTricker servers.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Ad Networks

The free tier of Anju Bajaranji Cal displays advertisements (such as banners or interstitial placements via Google AdMob or third-party ad SDKs) to support ongoing development.

  • Advertising Data: Third-party ad networks may automatically collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, device model, operating system version, app usage/ad-interaction events, and approximate location (derived from IP) for ad delivery, frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention.
  • Ad Personalization Choices: Depending on your region and consent settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Upgrading to Anju Bajaranji Cal Pro removes all advertisements from the app interface.
  • Partner Policies: For more details on how Google handles ad data, review Google Advertising Policies.

3. Currency Converter & Network Requests

When you open or refresh the Currency Converter tool, the app performs an HTTPS request to a public exchange-rate API (open.er-api.com) to retrieve the latest currency conversion rates.

No personal identifiers, calculation history, or sensitive user data are sent with exchange-rate requests. If the device is offline, built-in reference rates are used automatically.

4. Premium Subscriptions & Razorpay Payments

Anju Bajaranji Cal offers optional Monthly and Yearly Pro subscriptions. Payments are processed through Razorpay (and verified via server-side HMAC-SHA256 signature verification before unlocking Premium features).

  • Payment credentials such as UPI IDs, debit/credit card numbers, or netbanking passwords are handled directly by Razorpay and are never collected or stored by AiTricker.
  • Only non-sensitive transaction metadata (such as Order ID, Payment ID, and verification receipt status) is used to confirm and maintain your Pro entitlement.
  • Review Razorpay's Privacy Policy for details on how payment data is secured.

5. App Permissions

We follow strict least-privilege permission practices:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Required to fetch live currency exchange rates, load advertisements on the Free tier, and verify subscription payments.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — Used solely for optional tactile haptic feedback when pressing calculator keys.

All button click sound effects are synthesized locally in real time using Android AudioTrack. The app does not request access to your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Photos/Media Storage, or Precise GPS Location.

6. Children's Privacy

Anju Bajaranji Cal is a general-audience utility application and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your local data at all times:

  • Delete individual items or tap Clear All inside the app's History tab to erase saved calculations immediately.
  • Clear app storage from Android System Settings or uninstall Anju Bajaranji Cal to permanently delete all locally stored Room and DataStore data.
